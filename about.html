My story

My work started with a simple observation: small businesses lose hours every day to repetitive questions, messy reports and stock nobody can track. I build the systems that take that work away.

Every project follows the same five steps:

  1. Understand the problem — what is actually going wrong, and for whom.
  2. Study the current process — how the work happens today, step by step.
  3. Design a better solution — simpler, with less repetitive work.
  4. Build and connect the system — AI, software and the tools the business already uses.
  5. Test, improve and measure — check it works, then keep making it better.
