AI customer support system

Designed
The problem

Customer questions arrive through chat, email and phone. Teams spend too much time answering the same questions and searching for information.

What I created

AI assistants that can understand questions, find the correct information, complete approved tasks and transfer difficult cases to a person.

How it helps

Customers receive faster answers while employees spend more time on complicated cases.

My role: designed the assistants and the rules for when a person takes over.

In simple words

  1. A customer asks a question on chat, email or phone.
  2. The assistant understands the question and looks up the correct information.
  3. It answers, or completes an approved task such as checking an order.
  4. If the case is difficult or sensitive, it passes everything to a person.

Technical details

  • AI models: large language models answering only from approved business content.
  • Integrations: chat, email and phone channels connected through APIs.
  • Security & approvals: the assistant can only complete tasks the business has approved; sensitive actions always need a person.
  • Records: every conversation is logged so answers can be checked and improved.

ReportFlow

What I created

How it helps

My role: designing and building ReportFlow.

Accounting and Tally connection

Built
The problem

Accounting information stored on an office computer was difficult to access and synchronize with an online platform.

What I created

A system that securely connects the desktop accounting application with a cloud-based business platform.

How it helps

It allows information to stay synchronized while employees continue using their familiar accounting software.

My role: worked on the connection between the two systems.

In simple words

  1. The team keeps working in Tally on the office computer, as before.
  2. The connection reads the accounting information safely.
  3. It sends the information to the online platform and keeps both sides matching.

Customer feedback intelligence

Analysis complete
The problem

Important customer problems were hidden inside hundreds of thousands of conversations.

What I created

A process that reads and organizes customer feedback, identifies repeated problems and supports recommendations with real customer evidence.

How it helps

Business teams can see what customers are struggling with and decide what to fix first.

278,000+customer-service records studied

My role: created the analysis process and the recommendations it produced.

In simple words

  1. Customer conversations are collected from the service channels.
  2. Each conversation is read and sorted by the problem it describes.
  3. Repeated problems are counted and ranked, with real customer quotes as evidence.
  4. Teams get a clear list of what to fix first — and why.

Technical details

  • AI models: language models classify conversations into a fixed set of problem categories.
  • Evidence: every recommendation links back to the original customer records behind it.
  • Checks: samples are reviewed by a person so the categories stay accurate at scale.

Business data automation

Delivered
The problem

Important information was spread across customer-service, sales, shipping and reporting tools.

What I created

Connections between systems so information could be collected, updated and reported with less manual effort.

How it helps

Teams save time and receive more consistent information.

My role: connected the systems and automated the updates.

In simple words
